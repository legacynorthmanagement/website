About Legacy North

Legacy North Management provides professional condominium management services with a focus on clear communication, sound financial administration and practical support for Boards of Directors.

We work collaboratively with condominium communities to coordinate operations, support informed decision-making and help protect the long-term interests of each corporation.

Our approach

Responsive, transparent and tailored to the priorities and needs of each condominium community.

Leadership

James North, OLCM

President, Legacy North Management

James is an Ontario Licensed Condominium Manager with hands-on experience supporting condominium corporations throughout Niagara Region and surrounding communities. He founded Legacy North Management to provide Boards with responsive, practical and accountable management.

James remains directly involved in each community he manages, working closely with Boards, owners and service providers to oversee daily operations, financial planning, major projects and long-term priorities.

Management services

Reliable administrative, operational and governance support for condominium corporations.

Board Support

Meeting coordination, agendas, minutes, governance support and assistance implementing Board decisions.

Financial Administration

Budget preparation, financial reporting, arrears oversight and responsible reserve fund planning.

Property Operations

Contractor coordination, maintenance oversight, inspections and day-to-day operational management.

Capital Projects

Support with major repairs, tendering, contractor communication and project coordination.

Owner Communication

Clear notices, timely correspondence and professional handling of owner and resident concerns.

Compliance

Assistance with condominium legislation, governing documents, rules, records and annual meeting requirements.
